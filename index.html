🌟 Quick exam reminders

Little things that make a big difference

👂
Listening
Grab the main idea first — details come on the second pass. Use context clues, not panic.
✍️
Writing
Use connectors like además, sin embargo, por lo tanto — they signal sophisticated thinking.
🎤
Speaking
Fill silence with fillers: bueno…, es decir…, o sea… — native speakers do it too!
📖
Reading
Scan for cognates and root words first. Every unknown word has context neighbors — use them.
