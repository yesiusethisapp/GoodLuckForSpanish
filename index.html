🌟 Quick reminders from someone cheering you on

👂
Interpretive Listening
Read the questions before the audio plays so you know what to listen for. Main idea first, details second.
📰
Interpretive Reading
Scan for the author's purpose and tone first. For paired sources, look for how they agree or contrast.
✉️
Email Reply (Interpersonal)
Use a formal register. Ask at least one question back. Greet and sign off properly — it matters for scoring!
🎤
Presentational Speaking
Integrate all three sources and cite them clearly: "Según la fuente número dos…" fills silence AND earns points.
✍️
Presentational Writing
Structure: intro → source 1 → source 2 → source 3 → your opinion. Use connectors like sin embargo, además, por lo tanto.
💬
Simulated Conversation
20 seconds goes fast — start speaking immediately. Use fillers to bridge: bueno, o sea, es decir, pues…
